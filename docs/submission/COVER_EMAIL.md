Please find my submission, FINN, for the AI Engineer Build Challenge.

I regularly build financial-data and investment-related tools independently of assessments, so I enjoyed working on a problem I would use as an investor: understanding what changed, what deserves investigation, and where the evidence comes from.

I prioritized reliable source mappings, explicit dates and missing-data handling. Financial calculations stay deterministic and testable; Gemini is used selectively for interpretation over bounded evidence. Integrating the frontend, backend, persistent database and inference provider into a public application was a useful part of the build, including working through free-tier storage and deployment constraints.

I deliberately kept the scope focused: a filing brief, configurable EOD observations and research for selected issuers and periods. FINN does not claim comprehensive coverage, live trading or unsupported forecasts. Its interface makes historical data and source limitations visible.

My longer-term interest is in helping investors understand financial statements, alongside shorter-interval data and market-structure research, potentially extending to commodities and futures.
