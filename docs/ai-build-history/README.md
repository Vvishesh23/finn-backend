## Publication treatment

Redactions mark personal filesystem paths, local shell identity, private email addresses, deployment-specific preview URLs and credential-like content. Images/attachment bodies, tool arguments/results, ambient UI metadata and internal reasoning are omitted and identified. Full financial PDFs, original assessment documents, account screenshots and raw private exports are not published. Public production/repository links remain. Historical instructions/test counts are preserved as historical records, including corrected instructions; they are not current setup advice.

No separate ChatGPT conversation was synthesized, and no missing dialogue was invented. Checkpoint/test evidence exists in the private development checkout, but these curated documents are not a replacement for an independently exported original. The submission email remains a draft.
